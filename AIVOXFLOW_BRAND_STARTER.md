# AIVOXFLOW --- Brand Starter

## Brand core

**Name:** AIVOXFLOW\
**Domain target:** aivoxflow.com\
**Primary tagline:** Intelligence in Motion.\
**Supporting line:** Discover AI. Master Tools. Automate More.

## Positioning

A global, practical technology and AI publication that helps readers
discover useful tools, understand how they work, compare options, and
apply automation to real problems.

## Audience

-   Individuals exploring AI tools and productivity software
-   Creators, freelancers, and small businesses
-   Developers and technical professionals
-   Readers who want clear, practical explanations rather than hype

## Editorial promise

Every article should help the reader do at least one concrete thing:
choose a tool, complete a task, understand a technical concept, compare
alternatives, or make a better-informed decision.

## Voice

-   Clear, useful, specific, and internationally understandable
-   Confident without exaggerated promises
-   Transparent about limitations, uncertainty, sponsorship, affiliate
    links, and whether a product was actually tested
-   Define technical terms when first used; explain acronyms
-   Prefer evidence and practical examples over hype

## Visual direction

-   Primary background: deep navy
-   Accent: electric blue
-   Base: white and restrained neutral gray
-   Use a simple geometric "flow / connected signal" motif; avoid
    generic robot heads, excessive circuit-board clichés, tiny details,
    and heavy glow.
-   Logo should work as a horizontal wordmark and a compact icon; test
    at favicon size and on light/dark backgrounds.
-   Typography should prioritize readability and broad Latin/Arabic
    language coverage when Arabic launches.
-   Maintain WCAG-conscious contrast and visible focus states.

## Initial tagline usage

**Logo lockup:** AIVOXFLOW\
**Primary tagline:** Intelligence in Motion.\
**Hero supporting copy:** Discover AI tools, learn practical workflows,
and make technology work for you.

## Content taxonomy

1.  AI Tools
2.  Tutorials
3.  Software & Web
4.  Automation & Agents
5.  AI News
6.  Comparisons

## Launch scope

Start with English. Add Spanish and French after the editorial workflow
can consistently produce accurate, useful English content; add German,
Arabic, and Portuguese in later phases. Each language needs a genuinely
localized, crawlable URL and quality review.

## Brand/legal checks before major spend

Domain registration and trademark clearance are separate. Verify the
exact name and confusingly similar marks in relevant jurisdictions and
classes before investing heavily. This starter is a creative direction,
not a legal clearance opinion.


## Phase-one deployment
The first version runs on free hosting and a free domain/subdomain for development and testing. Keep the eventual custom domain configurable; see `AIVOXFLOW_FREE_PHASE_REQUIREMENTS.md`.
